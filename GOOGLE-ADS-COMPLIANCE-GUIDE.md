# Google Ads Compliance Guide — CSC Bir Sujra

Updated: 6 July 2026

## Important
Website changes reduce policy risk but do not guarantee Google Ads approval. Google reviews the ad, keywords, account, business verification, destination and external information.

## Campaign types

### Safer general local campaign
Use `/ads/local-digital-services.html` and promote only printing, scanning, photocopy, photo resizing, PDF, typing and general document preparation. Do not put PAN, Aadhaar, Voter ID, passport, certificate, scheme, banking, insurance or loan keywords in this campaign.

### Government documents/services campaign
PAN, Voter ID, passport, certificates, licences, benefits and similar direct-acquisition ads can require Google Government Documents and Services certification plus Advertiser Verification. A website disclaimer does not remove this requirement. Only claim authorized-provider status when your domain is explicitly linked by an official government website as authorized for that service.

### Financial services campaign
Do not advertise banking, insurance, loan, investment, pension-enrolment or similar financial keywords until the required India Financial Services Verification/provider authorization is complete.

## Before publishing ads
1. Deploy on the exact HTTPS domain shown in the ad.
2. Confirm all pages return HTTP 200 on mobile and desktop and are crawlable by AdsBot.
3. Keep +91 70274 94674 visible as text on the landing page for call verification.
4. Complete Google Advertiser Verification with business details matching the payment profile and website.
5. Never use “official,” “government approved,” “guaranteed,” “100% approval,” “instant,” or fake urgency.
6. Set ad final URL to the same domain; avoid redirects to another domain. WhatsApp may be a CTA but should not be the ad final URL.
7. Keep Privacy, Terms, Pricing, Payment Policy and Transparency links visible.
8. Do not collect Aadhaar/PAN/bank/health numbers over HTTP. This site does not collect them at all.
9. If Google Ads/Analytics tags are added, update Privacy Policy and implement applicable consent controls. Never send PII in URL parameters, conversion labels or remarketing lists.
10. Review actual service availability and authorization before advertising any service.

## Recommended ad wording for general campaign
- Local Print, Scan & PDF Help
- Photo Resize & Document Printing
- Digital Document Help Near Bir Sujra
- Call or WhatsApp for Availability

Avoid government-document keywords unless certification is approved.
